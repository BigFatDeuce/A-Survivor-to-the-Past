# Credits

**A Survivor to the Past** is an unofficial fan game. All graphics, music and sound effects from *The Legend of Zelda* series (and Mario and Shy Guy, from the *Mario* series) belong to **Nintendo**. *Sonic the Hedgehog* belongs to **SEGA**, *Among Us* to **Innersloth**, Simon Belmont (*Castlevania*) to **Konami**, Vivi (*Final Fantasy IX*) to **Square Enix**, and the *Clair Obscur: Expedition 33* characters to **Sandfall Interactive**. This project is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by any of these companies.

The sprite sheets used by the game were found on [The Spriters Resource](https://www.spriters-resource.com/). Many thanks to the people who ripped, edited or drew them. The names below are the ones written on each sheet; a sheet with no name on it is marked "not stated".

If you are the author of one of these sheets and want a credit corrected, added or removed, please open an issue.

## Project files

- `assets/source/Derived - *.png`: frames cut out of the sheets above and repacked by `research/tools/gen_chars.py`, `research/tools/gen_chars_v061.py` and `research/tools/gen_chars_v062.py`, so the walk cycles line up, item icons cut by `research/tools/gen_items_v062.py`, and the Frozen Lake props (ice floes and stakes cut out of the Dark World map, water removed) by `research/tools/gen_ice_props_v062.py`. The pixels are unchanged, with these exceptions: the Crewmate colors other than red are palette swaps using the colors of the original game; the shadows drawn under Sheik and Simon Belmont are removed (the game draws its own); the blue lantern and Vaati's Jar icons are hue shifts of the lantern and Gust Jar; Sheik's back and side poses are new drawings, added to a copy of the sheet (`assets/source/Custom - Sheik (Oldskoolmario, back and side added).png`).
- Wardrobe outfits made by `research/tools/gen_wardrobe_v064.py` (`assets/source/Derived - <outfit>.png`): the drawings are the work of the authors credited above for each character (Adult Link: Oldskoolmario and Redfusion; NES Link: Mister Mike; Toon Link: Oldskoolmario; Mario: AquariusAlexx; Shy Guy: yesilovenachos; Sonic: Ragnarockerbunny and Blueblur64); only the colors were changed for this game (Goron and Zora tunics, green NES tunic, red and blue Toon Link, Mario in Luigi's colors, blue / green / yellow Shy Guy, Super Sonic). The NES Link red ring outfit and Bunny Link use the sheets' own colors.
- The stage props, title screen background and logo were prepared for this project from Nintendo artwork.
- The atlas in `assets/gen/` is built from all of the above by `tools/forge`.
